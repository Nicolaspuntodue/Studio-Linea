The space between the lines

We design residences in stone, shadow and light. Scroll to walk through Casa Linea, our latest private villa.

01 — Threshold

A 3.4 m pivot door in blackened steel.

Lime plaster, honed travertine and a single slot of light drawing you inward.

02 — Gallery

The line becomes the plan.

Smoked oak meets microcement along a 24 m axis; light grazes, never glares.

03 — Living

Double height, single gesture.

A monolithic stone hearth anchors a room that dissolves into the garden.

04 — Kitchen

Nero Marquina, cut as one.

A waterfall island in book-matched marble, framed by fluted oak and bronze.

05 — Dining

Golden hour, by design.

The table is oriented to the olive tree, so the evening light sets the table.

06 — Stair

Travertine, suspended.

Cantilevered treads and a blade of skylight: a sculpture you can climb.

07 — Suite

Quiet, measured in wool and linen.

Acoustic upholstered walls, hidden light, and the sea beyond the olive groves.

08 — Bath

Carved from a single block.

A 2.1-tonne marble tub set in a room of continuous stone.

09 — Horizon

And then, only the line.

Basalt, water and sky meet at the edge where Casa Linea ends.

Manifesto

We believe luxury is not addition but precision. A shadow gap of three millimetres. A wall that holds the evening light. A room that asks nothing of you. Every Studio Linea interior begins with a single line, and we refuse to draw a second until the first is right.

What truly matters to us

Co-creation

Your home should be an extension of you. Every brief begins with listening: how you wake, host, read and rest.

Craft

Thirty-two Italian workshops, one standard. Stone, bronze and timber detailed to the millimetre and finished by hand.

Longevity

Certified materials, local supply chains and designs meant to age, not date. The most sustainable room is the one you keep.
